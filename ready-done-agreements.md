# Ready and Done Agreements

These are first-version team agreements for the Policy Assistant Pilot. Keep them short and revise them at the next checkpoint.

## Definition of Ready (DoR)

A story can move from **Backlog** to **Ready** when:

- [ ] The role, goal, and benefit are clear.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are written and testable.
- [ ] Known dependencies are listed (see `epic-map.md`).
- [ ] Open questions and assumptions are visible.
- [ ] The story is small enough for one delivery cycle.
- [ ] Required source material (approved policy documents, disclaimer text) is available or has a named owner.

DoR is a collaboration aid, not a wall: discovery work may start before every item is checked.

## Definition of Done (DoD)

A story can move from **Review** to **Done** when:

- [ ] All acceptance criteria are met and checked by someone other than the person who built it.
- [ ] Only approved policy documents are used.
- [ ] Fallback behavior is tested (for example, "No approved source found").
- [ ] Suggested answers are reviewed against agreed example questions.
- [ ] Human escalation and review paths work.
- [ ] User-facing text, including the disclaimer, is reviewed (legal review where needed).
- [ ] Logging for audit is in place.
- [ ] Known limitations are recorded.
- [ ] Results are labeled as pilot, mock, or validated.
